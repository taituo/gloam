The rule

One pen, held at 45°

Gloam pretends every pixel was laid by a broad nib. Strokes that travel ↗ come out thin; strokes that travel ↘ come out thick. That single rule settles every corner, dot and diagonal in the font.

What’s inside

Built for the screen it lives on

glyphs

Latin-1 and the core of Extended-A: Finnish, Nordic, German, French, Polish, Czech, Turkish, Romanian, Hungarian, Icelandic.

Aa

Two families

Gloam is the clean outline. Gloam Lantern is a colour font — shadow, halo, fill and a highlight on every top edge.

Six palettes

Ember, Moss, Frost, Ghost, Blood, Bone — baked into the font. Switch with one line of CSS: font-palette.

16×

Pixel-true

1 px = 100 units on a 1600 UPM grid. Set it at 16, 32, 48, 64… and every edge lands on a real pixel.

UI-ready

41 box-drawing, block and shade glyphs on a strict 8×16 cell, plus arrows, cursors, suits and six game icons.

Desktop, web and engine. Sources ship as plain ASCII bitmaps, so you can redraw a glyph in a text editor.
